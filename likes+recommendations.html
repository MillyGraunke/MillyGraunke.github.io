Recommendations

Books have always been one of my favorite ways to explore new ideas, worlds, and perspectives.
On this page, I share the books, authors, and series I've enjoyed, along with recommendations for books
I think are worth reading. My tastes range across different genres, from thought-provoking nonfiction
immersive fantasy, science fiction, mysteries, and everything in between. Whether you're
looking for your next great read or just curious about the kinds of books I enjoy, I hope you'll find
something here that inspires you to pick up a new book.

Summer Reads 2026:

Jennifer Lynn Barnes has become one of my favorite authors because every book I've read by her
has been impossible to put down. I loved The Inheritance Games series for its clever riddles,
unexpected twists, and the mystery surrounding the Hawthorne family. After finishing that series,
and The Brothers Hawthorne I couldn't wait to read The Grandest Game series, and The Gilded Blade
was such an exciting end. Barnes does an amazing job creating smart, resourceful characters and stories
that keep me guessing until the very end. I also like how every clue matters, making it fun to try to
solve the mystery alongside the characters. So far, I've read every book in these series except
Games Untold, and Same Forward as Backward, and I'm looking forward to reading it because
I know it will add even more to a world of characters I've really enjoyed.

Kiera Cass's The Selection series is one of my favorite dystopian romance series
because it combines royal life, competition, and character growth into a story
that is hard to put down. I enjoyed following America Singer as she unexpectedly
becomes part of the Selection and has to balance her own dreams with the
responsibilities that come with competing for the prince's heart. While
the romance is a big part of the story, I also liked learning about the world
of Illéa and seeing the characters grow through difficult choices and challenges.
After finishing the main series, I also really enjoyed Happily Ever After
because it gave me the chance to revisit my favorite characters and read stories
from different points of view. It made the world feel even more complete and
answered questions I still had after finishing the novels. I would recommend
the entire series, including Happily Ever After, to anyone who enjoys romance,
dystopian fiction, and stories with memorable characters and satisfying endings.
I have not yet read The Crown + Heir but with her writing I am sure I will love it.
