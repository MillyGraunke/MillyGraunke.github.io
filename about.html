Hi! I’m Milly,
a multi-passionate creator and problem-solver from Chicago with a love for both the physical and the technical. My interests span from competitive sports like judo, baseball, football, and softball to hands-on crafts such as metalworking and needlepoint. I’m equally drawn to the precision of math, the creativity of photography, and the innovation of coding and robotics. I approach every project with curiosity, discipline, and a drive to master new skills — whether that’s building something tangible, solving a complex problem, or capturing a moment in time. My goal is to combine creativity and technical expertise to make ideas come alive.
