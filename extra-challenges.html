Extra Challenges

Here are some challenges you can face on a regular basis that improve who you are.

  • 1. Smile at 5 People Today: Start your day by spotting five people and giving them a genuine smile. This simple gesture can brighten someone’s mood and set a positive tone for the rest of the day.
  • 2. 28 Days of Kindness: Choose one act of kindness each day for 28 consecutive days. It can be as simple as holding the door, complimenting someone, or leaving a note. This builds consistency and helps kindness become a habit
  • 3. Create a Kindness Jar: Fill a jar with uplifting notes, encouraging words, or small tokens of appreciation. Leave it in a shared space at work, school, or home for others to take when they need a boost.
  • 4. Pay-It-Forward Chain: At a café or drive-thru, pay for the person behind you. See how far the chain of kindness can go—this can inspire others to do the same.
  • 5. Leave a “Hidden Treasure”: Write a kind note or draw a cheerful doodle and tuck it into a library book, shopping cart, or seat on public transport for a stranger to find.
  • 6.Compliment with a Twist: Go beyond the usual “you’re pretty” and compliment on something less obvious, like their patience, creativity, or ability to stay calm.
  • 7. Help a Younger Student or Sibling: Offer to help with homework, show them a new game, or share a skill you know. This fosters mentorship and strengthens relationships.
  • 8. Make a Card for a Family Member: Write a heartfelt note or draw a picture for someone close. Even a small gesture can bring joy and strengthen bonds.
Tip: You can combine these challenges into a personal kindness calendar or share them with friends and family to make it a group effort. The key is to keep it simple, consistent, and intentional—small acts can have big impacts.
